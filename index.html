The 4 Pillars of SARVAVIDYA

Engineered to deliver an intelligent, closed-loop educational lifecycle.

1. AI Recommendation Engine

Powered by student mastery vectors, BKT probability shifts, and prerequisite graphs. Automatically distributes guidance across 6 actionable categories:

• Study Concept • Practice Drills • Revise Lessons • Advanced Mode • Fix Prerequisites • Continue Path

2. Multi-Subject Learning Hub

True multi-disciplinary platform with isolated knowledge models across Programming, Mathematics, Quantum Computing, and Engineering disciplines:

3. SARVA AI Assistant

A context-aware learning companion attached directly to the student's active track. Features deep syllabus fallback, step-by-step math breakdowns, and code troubleshooting.

> SARVA AI: "I noticed you struggled with Loops. Let's trace an iteration example together..."
